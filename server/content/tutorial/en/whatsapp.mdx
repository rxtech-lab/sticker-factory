<TutorialStep id="open" title="Open the pack">

Open a prepared pack and tap Add to WhatsApp. The app downloads its prepared sticker files. If files are missing from your own pack, edit and save the pack to prepare them.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Open the pack" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="parts" title="Review the parts">

Still and animated stickers are separated. Large packs are split into supported parts. Review any skipped stickers and wait for downloading to finish.

<TutorialMedia id="whatsapp-20260916" caption="Review the parts" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="emoji" title="Choose sticker emoji">

Choose the emoji associated with each sticker before sending the pack. Install WhatsApp if the app says it is unavailable, then return to this screen.

<TutorialMedia id="whatsapp-20260916" caption="Choose sticker emoji" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="handoff" title="Add one part at a time">

Tap a ready part to open WhatsApp and finish adding it there. Return to Winky for any remaining parts. The final import is confirmed in WhatsApp.

<TutorialMedia id="whatsapp-20260916" caption="Add one part at a time" />

</TutorialStep>
