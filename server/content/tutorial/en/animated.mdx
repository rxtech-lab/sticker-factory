<TutorialStep id="choose" title="Choose Animated">

In Create, describe your idea first, tap Next, and choose Animated. Continue through style and optional themes to the animation settings, then review your choices. Describe the character and the motion you want, such as a small wave or a gentle bounce. Add reference images when the look matters.

<TutorialMedia id="create-animated-20260916" caption="Choose Animated" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="reference" title="Check the visual reference">

The assistant proposes a plan and a static visual reference for generated artwork. Check the character, composition and style. Ask for changes before confirming.

<TutorialMedia id="plan-20260916" caption="Check the visual reference" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="build" title="Confirm the animation plan">

Review the motion and timing, then confirm the plan. The approved artwork is prepared for animation. More generated artwork can use more credits.

<TutorialMedia id="plan-20260916" caption="Confirm the animation plan" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="preview" title="Watch before accepting">

Open the candidate preview and watch the movement. Accept the version you like, or explain what should change. An animated project needs motion before it can be published as an animation.

<TutorialMedia id="sticker-20260916" caption="Watch before accepting" />

</TutorialStep>
