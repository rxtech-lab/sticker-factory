<TutorialStep id="open" title="Choose Telegram">

Open a prepared pack and tap Add to Telegram. The app downloads the prepared files; missing files in your own pack can be prepared by editing and saving it.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Choose Telegram" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="parts" title="Check your export">

Stills and animations are exported in Telegram-compatible formats and separate parts. Review the list and any skipped stickers before continuing.

<TutorialMedia id="telegram-20260916" caption="Check your export" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="emoji" title="Set emoji and get ready">

Choose the emoji for each sticker. If Telegram is not installed, install it and return. Wait until the export part is ready before opening Telegram.

<TutorialMedia id="telegram-20260916" caption="Set emoji and get ready" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="handoff" title="Finish in Telegram">

Open a ready part in Telegram, choose the pack name when asked, and finish the import there. Return to Winky to add additional parts.

<TutorialMedia id="telegram-20260916" caption="Finish in Telegram" />

</TutorialStep>
