<TutorialStep id="browse" title="Find a pack">

Open Sticker Packs. Browse or search for something you like. My packs holds packs you created; Browse shows available packs from creators.

<TutorialMedia id="packs-20260916" caption="Find a pack" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="preview" title="Look inside">

Open a pack and tap a sticker to preview it. Controllable stickers expose their available settings; your choices do not edit the creator’s artwork.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Look inside" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="install" title="Add a pack to your library">

Use the pack’s add control to install another creator’s pack. Your own packs offer Edit pack instead because your stickers are already in your library.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Add a pack to your library" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="share" title="Take it to a messenger">

The pack page offers WhatsApp and Telegram export. Open the matching chapter for a walkthrough. A controllable sticker is rendered into the format the messenger accepts.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Take it to a messenger" />

</TutorialStep>
