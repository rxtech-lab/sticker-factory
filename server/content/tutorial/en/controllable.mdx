<TutorialStep id="enable" title="Switch on moods and poses">

Describe your idea, choose Animated, select a style and optional themes, then turn on Switchable moods and poses on the animation screen. The character can offer selectable expressions and poses after generation.

<TutorialMedia id="create-controllable-20260916" caption="Switch on moods and poses" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="variety" title="Choose pose variety">

Min, Medium, High and Ultra request 2, 3, 5 and 8 selectable poses per character, respectively. Higher presets need more generated artwork and cost more. Start with Medium if you are exploring.

<TutorialMedia id="create-controllable-20260916" caption="Choose pose variety" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="build" title="Confirm the character">

Review the visual reference and plan before building. Check the character and the proposed poses. Accept the finished candidate when it matches your idea.

<TutorialMedia id="plan-20260916" caption="Confirm the character" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="controls" title="Try the controls">

Open a controllable sticker. Choose the available mood or pose, adjust Speed, or turn Animate off to pick a still frame. Some stickers also offer a sequence of actions.

<TutorialMedia id="controls-20260916" caption="Try the controls" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="apply" title="Keep the version you like">

Apply saves your chosen settings; Cancel leaves saved settings unchanged. Reset returns the controls to their defaults. Exported images and videos contain a rendered result, not the interactive controls.

<TutorialMedia id="controls-20260916" caption="Keep the version you like" />

</TutorialStep>
