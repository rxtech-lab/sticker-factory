<TutorialStep id="choose" title="Start with Static">

Open Create from Library, describe your idea, then tap Next and choose Static. Continue to choose a style and, optionally, themes before reviewing your choices.

<TutorialMedia id="create-static-20260916" caption="Start with Static" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="describe" title="Describe your idea">

Describe the subject, expression, colors and style. For example: a cheerful corgi in a yellow raincoat with a thick white sticker border.

<TutorialMedia id="create-prompt-20260916" caption="Describe your idea" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="references" title="Add references if they help">

On the idea screen, tap Add to attach up to eight reference images. Use Back to return there if you have already moved on. Photos help preserve a particular character or style. They are optional; review your selection before starting.

<TutorialMedia id="create-references-20260916" caption="Add references if they help" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="review" title="Review, build and accept">

On the review screen, check your idea, type, style and themes. Tap any section to edit it, then tap Generate one candidate to start the project. Review the assistant’s plan in chat, confirm when it looks right, then accept or reject the candidate. Generation uses credits; check the plan before confirming.

<TutorialMedia id="plan-20260916" caption="Review, build and accept" />

</TutorialStep>
