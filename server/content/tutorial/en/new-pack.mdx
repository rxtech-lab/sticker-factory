<TutorialStep id="name" title="Start a new pack">

In Sticker Packs, tap New pack. Give your collection a name and an optional description.

<TutorialMedia id="new-pack-20260916" caption="Start a new pack" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="select" title="Choose published stickers">

Add stickers from your published library. If a sticker is missing, finish exporting and publishing it first. Reorder the selected stickers to tell your story.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-picker-20260916" caption="Choose published stickers" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="save" title="Save or publish">

Choose Save as draft to keep working, or Create and publish to make the pack available. Wait while the app creates the pack and updates your list.

<TutorialMedia id="new-pack-20260916" caption="Save or publish" />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep id="prepare" title="Prepare it for sharing">

When messenger files are missing, the app prepares them on this device after saving. Keep the preparation screen open until it finishes. The pack page then offers WhatsApp and Telegram.

<TutorialMedia id="pack-detail-20260916" caption="Prepare it for sharing" />

</TutorialStep>
